Websites for landscapers in the Nashville area

You’re busy on job sites, not websites. We build your site, host it, and keep it up to date, so you never have to touch it.

Why it matters

When a homeowner hears your name, they look you up. A bad website, or no website, makes you look like you’re out of business. A clear one shows your work, the services you offer and the towns you serve, and puts your phone number one tap away.

What you get

  • A site branded to you: your name, colors, photos and services.
  • Pages that show visitors what you do, where you work, and how to reach you.
  • Easy to read on a phone, with tap-to-call and tap-to-email buttons.
  • Hosting and security, with no extra hosting fees.
  • Updates handled for you. New photos, a new service, a new season: email or text us, and we change it based on your plan.

See an example

Fernhill Landscapes is our example site for a landscaper. It’s calm and outdoorsy, with services, a simple how-it-works, service towns, and an estimate form.

Fernhill Landscapes is a concept website for a made-up business, not a client project. Its contact details are fake, and its buttons and forms don’t send anything.

How it works

  1. Design it together. We build a mockup branded to your business, then work out the details with you. You never see the code.
  2. Send your logo and photos. We put them into your design and make it yours.
  3. Integrations. We work out your domain, plus payments and integrations based on your plan.
  4. Go live. We host it and keep it up to date.
  5. Updates. We handle all the updates based on your plan. Just email or text us what you want.

What it costs

  • Starter, $49 per month. A one-page website, branded to your business. Hosting and security. SEO management. Small changes when you need them. Tap-to-call and tap-to-email buttons.
  • Pro, $99 per month. Everything in Starter. Up to 5 pages. Scheduling and service-payment integration. A big change every week, and small changes any time.
  • Premier, $199 per month. Everything in Pro. Up to 10 pages. Weekly website status updates. Any integrations. Any reasonable change, any time.

Every plan has a 3-month minimum, then it’s month-to-month. There are no extra hosting fees. See the plans.
