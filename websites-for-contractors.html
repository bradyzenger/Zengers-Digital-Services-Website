Websites for contractors in the Nashville area

For electricians, handymen, pressure washers, cleaners and other trades and home-service businesses. We build your site, host it, and keep it up to date, so you never have to touch it.

Why it matters

Customers look you up before they call. A bad website, or no website, makes you look like you’re out of business. And if a visitor can’t find your phone number or your services in a few seconds, they leave. A clear website shows what you do and where you work, and puts your phone number one tap away.

What you get

  • A site branded to you: your name, colors, photos and services.
  • Pages that show visitors what you do, where you work, and how to reach you.
  • Easy to read on a phone, with tap-to-call and tap-to-email buttons.
  • Hosting and security, with no extra hosting fees.
  • On the Pro plan, scheduling and service-payment integration.
  • Updates handled for you. A new service, new photos, new hours: email or text us, and we change it based on your plan.

How it works

  1. Design it together. We build a mockup branded to your business, then work out the details with you. You never see the code.
  2. Send your logo and photos. We make it yours: your name, colors, photos and services.
  3. Integrations. We work out your domain, plus payments and integrations based on your plan.
  4. Go live. We host it and keep it up to date.
  5. Updates. We handle all the updates based on your plan. Just email or text us what you want.

What it costs

  • Starter, $49 per month. A one-page website, branded to your business. Hosting and security. Small changes when you need them. Tap-to-call and tap-to-email buttons.
  • Pro, $99 per month. Everything in Starter. Up to 5 pages. Scheduling and service-payment integration. A big change every week, and small changes any time.
  • Premier, $199 per month. Everything in Pro. Up to 10 pages. Weekly website status updates. Any reasonable change, any time.

Every plan has a 3-month minimum, then it’s month-to-month. There are no extra hosting fees. See the plans.
