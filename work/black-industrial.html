BLACK Industrial & Safety Supply

The business

BLACK Industrial & Safety Supply sells industrial and safety supplies from its showroom in Hammond, Indiana. Its customers are energy companies, manufacturers and industrial contractors in Northern Indiana and Northern Illinois.

BLACK is part of the ZENGERS Family of Brands. ZENGERS Digital Services is a division of ZENGERS Inc., so this was a job for a company in our own family.

What the site needed to do

BLACK wanted a new site in place of its old one, not a refresh. The visitors are the people who buy for a plant: plant managers, purchasing, safety managers and maintenance staff. The main thing they should be able to do is send a request for quote, with the phone number one tap away on every page.

What we built

  • Home. A looping video of industrial work behind the headline (stock footage, with a pause button). A downtime meter that counts what a stopped line would have cost since the page opened, at an hourly rate the visitor sets. What BLACK supplies to each kind of customer, its services, and a checklist of what helps it find a hard-to-find part.
  • Request for quote. A form laid out like a packing slip, for a company, a contact and a parts list. It opens the visitor’s own email app with the list filled in.
  • Line card. 17 product categories, each with its featured brands, shortcuts at the top, and a button to print it or save it as a PDF.
  • Supplier setup. What a purchasing department needs to add BLACK as a supplier.

How it looks

Black and safety yellow come from BLACK’s name and its logo. The headings are stenciled, like the markings on a crate. The pages sit on concrete gray, with cards like label plates and a yellow-and-black hazard stripe.

The site makes no claims with made-up numbers. The downtime meter makes its point with the visitor’s own.
